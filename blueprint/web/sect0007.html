  • FDRep.character_leftRegular

\(\chi _{k[G]}(g) = |G|\) if \(g = 1\) and \(0\) otherwise.

Proof ▶

In the basis \(\{ \delta _h : h \in G\} \), the matrix of left translation by \(g\) is the permutation matrix for \(h \mapsto gh\). Its trace counts fixed points: \(h = gh\) iff \(g = 1\), in which case all \(|G|\) basis vectors are fixed.
