6.4 Sum-of-squares dimension formula

Corollary 30 Sum of squares of dimensions
✓
#

Uses

  • Theorem 29
  • Theorem 29

Lean declarations

  • PeterWeyl.sum_sq_dim_eq_card

Under the standing hypotheses, with \((d_i)_i\) the dimensions appearing in Theorem 29,

\[ |G| \; =\; \sum _{i {\lt} n} d_i^{\, 2}. \]
Proof ▶

Take \(\dim _k\) of both sides of the isomorphism in Theorem 29: the left side is \(\dim _k\, k[G] = |G|\) (Module.finrank_finsupp_self); the right side, by Module.finrank_pi_fintype and Module.finrank_matrix, is \(\sum _i \dim _k \mathrm{Mat}_{d_i}(k) = \sum _i d_i^2\).
