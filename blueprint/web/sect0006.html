  • IsSemisimpleModule.exists_end_algEquiv_pi_matrix_end

For any IsSemisimpleModule R M with Module.Finite R M, there exist \(n \in \mathbb N\), simple submodules \((S_i)_{i {\lt} n}\) of \(M\), and dimensions \((d_i)_{i {\lt} n}\) with each \(d_i \ne 0\), such that

\[ \mathrm{End}_R M \; \simeq _{R_0\text{-alg}}\; \prod _{i {\lt} n} \mathrm{Mat}_{d_i \times d_i}\bigl(\mathrm{End}_R S_i\bigr). \]
Proof ▶
