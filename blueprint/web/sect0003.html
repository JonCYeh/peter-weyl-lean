  • MonoidAlgebra.Submodule.instIsSemisimpleModule

Under the standing hypotheses (Definition 1), for every Module (MonoidAlgebra k G) V we have IsSemisimpleModule (MonoidAlgebra k G) V: every \(k[G]\)-submodule has a \(k[G]\)-complement.

Proof ▶

This is the registered instance MonoidAlgebra.Submodule.instIsSemisimpleModule in Mathlib.RepresentationTheory.Maschke, with hypotheses [Field k] [Group G] [Finite G] [NeZero (Nat.card G : k)]. The Mathlib proof uses LinearMap.equivariantProjection (averaging \(\pi \) over \(G\)) plus LinearMap.equivariantProjection_condition.

  • FDRep.isSemisimpleModule_asModule

For every \(V : \texttt{FDRep k G}\), V.ρ.asModule is a semisimple MonoidAlgebra k G-module and is finite as a \(k[G]\)-module.
