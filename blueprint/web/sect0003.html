1.3 The Hilbert space \(L^2(G)\)

Definition 9 \(L^2(G)\)
✓
#

Uses

  • Definition 3
  • Definition 2

Lean declarations

  • PeterWeyl.L2

\(L^2(G) := \texttt{Lp $\mathbb C$ 2 $\mu _G$}\), the Bochner \(L^2\) space of \(\mathbb C\)-valued square-integrable equivalence classes of functions on \(G\), with inner product \(\langle f, h\rangle := \int _G f(g)\overline{h(g)}\, d\mu _G(g)\).

Mathlib provides the structure (file Mathlib.MeasureTheory.Function.L2Space) including the inner-product instance L2.innerProductSpace, the norm \(\| f\| _{L^2}^2 = \int |f|^2 d\mu _G\), and completeness (MeasureTheory.Lp.completeSpace).
