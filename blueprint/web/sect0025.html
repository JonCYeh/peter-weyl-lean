The image of \(\mathcal A\) in \(L^2(G)\) under \(\iota _{C\to L^2}\) is dense in \(L^2(G)\).

Proof ▶

\(\mathcal A\) uniformly dense in \(C(G)\) (Theorem 79) gives \(\iota _{C\to L^2}(\mathcal A)\) dense in \(\iota _{C\to L^2}(C(G))\) in the \(L^2\) topology (Theorem 13). And \(\iota _{C\to L^2}(C(G))\) is dense in \(L^2(G)\) (Theorem 12).
