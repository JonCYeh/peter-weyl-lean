Lean declarations

  • Representation.multiplicity_eq_inner_char

Let \(V : \texttt{FDRep k G}\) and \(\xi \) a simple \(V_\xi : \texttt{FDRep k G}\). Write \(m_\xi \) for the multiplicity of \(V_\xi \) in the isotypic decomposition of \(V\) (Theorem 24). Then

\[ m_\xi \; =\; |G|^{-1}\sum _{g} \chi _V(g)\chi _{V_\xi }(g^{-1}) \; =\; \mathrm{finrank}_k\bigl(V_\xi \to V\bigr)_G. \]
Proof ▶

Decompose \(V \cong \bigoplus _\eta V_\eta ^{\oplus m_\eta }\) via Theorem 24. Then \(\mathrm{finrank}_k(V_\xi \to V)_G = \sum _\eta m_\eta \mathrm{finrank}_k(V_\xi \to V_\eta )_G = m_\xi \) by Theorem 12. The first equality is then Theorem 21.

  • FDRep.irreducible_iff_inner_self_eq_one

\(V : \texttt{FDRep k G}\) is simple iff \(|G|^{-1}\sum _g \chi _V(g)\chi _V(g^{-1}) = 1\).

Proof ▶

Decompose \(V \cong \bigoplus _\xi V_\xi ^{\oplus m_\xi }\) and expand. By Theorem 22, \(|G|^{-1}\sum _g \chi _V(g)\chi _V(g^{-1}) = \sum _\xi m_\xi ^2\). This integer is \(1\) iff exactly one \(m_\xi \) is \(1\) and the rest are \(0\), iff \(V \cong V_\xi \) is simple.

  • FDRep.iso_iff_character_eq

\(V \cong W\) in \(\texttt{FDRep k G}\) iff \(\chi _V = \chi _W\).

Proof ▶

\(\Rightarrow \): Lemma 19. \(\Leftarrow \): by Theorem 33 the multiplicities \(m_\xi (V)\) and \(m_\xi (W)\) depend only on the characters, so they coincide; uniqueness of the isotypic decomposition (Theorem 24) then forces \(V \cong W\).
