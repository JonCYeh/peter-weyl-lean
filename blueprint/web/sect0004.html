2.1 The structure

Definition 14 Continuous unitary representation
✓
#

Uses

  • Definition 1
  • Definition 2

Lean declarations

  • UnitaryRep

A continuous unitary representation of \(G\) on a complex Hilbert space \(H\) is a structure UnitaryRep G H bundling:

  • toMonoidHom : G \(\to \)* (H \(\to \)L[\(\mathbb C\)] H), the underlying group homomorphism into bounded operators;

  • isUnitary : \(\forall \) g : G, IsUnitary (toMonoidHom g), where IsUnitary T is shorthand for T.adjoint \(\circ \)L T = ContinuousLinearMap.id;

  • strongContinuous : \(\forall \) v : H, Continuous (fun g \(\Rightarrow \) toMonoidHom g v), strong-operator continuity.

We write \(\rho : \texttt{UnitaryRep G H}\) for such an object and \(\rho (g) v := \rho .\texttt{toMonoidHom}\ g\ v\).

Hypotheses on \(H\) throughout: [NormedAddCommGroup H] [InnerProductSpace \(\mathbb C\) H] [CompleteSpace H].

Theorem 15 \(\rho (g)\) preserves inner products

  • UnitaryRep.inner_apply

For \(\rho : \texttt{UnitaryRep G H}\), \(g : G\), and \(u, v : H\), \(\langle \rho (g) u, \rho (g) v\rangle = \langle u, v\rangle \).

Proof ▶

Compute \(\langle \rho (g)u, \rho (g)v\rangle = \langle \rho (g)^*\rho (g) u, v\rangle \), then use isUnitary to replace \(\rho (g)^*\rho (g)\) by \(\mathrm{Id}\).

  • UnitaryRep.norm_apply

\(\| \rho (g) v\| = \| v\| \) for all \(g, v\).

Proof ▶

\(\| \rho (g)v\| ^2 = \langle \rho (g)v, \rho (g)v\rangle = \langle v, v\rangle = \| v\| ^2\) by Theorem 15.

  • UnitaryRep.opNorm_apply_le_one

\(\| \rho (g)\| _{\mathrm{op}} \leq 1\).

Proof ▶

By Theorem 16, \(\| \rho (g)v\| \leq 1 \cdot \| v\| \) for every \(v\), so \(\| \rho (g)\| _\mathrm {op} \leq 1\) by the definition of operator norm.
