Let \(T : H \to _L H\) be such that \(T.\texttt{IsIntertwiner $\rho $ $\rho $}\), and \(\lambda \in \mathbb C\). Then \(\rho .\texttt{IsInvariant}\, \ker (T - \lambda \, \mathrm{Id})\).

Proof ▶

If \(T v = \lambda v\) then \(T(\rho (g) v) = \rho (g)(Tv) = \lambda \rho (g) v\).

  • PeterWeyl.UnitaryRep.IsIrreducible.compact_selfAdjoint_intertwiner_eq_smul_or_zero

Let \(\rho : \texttt{UnitaryRep G H}\) be irreducible, and \(A : H \to _L H\) compact, self-adjoint, with \(A.\texttt{IsIntertwiner $\rho $ $\rho $}\). Then either \(A = 0\), or there exists \(\lambda \in \mathbb R \setminus \{ 0\} \) with \(A = \lambda \cdot \mathrm{Id}\).

Proof ▶

If \(A \neq 0\), by Theorem 50 there exist \(\lambda \neq 0\) real and a non-zero finite-dimensional \(H_\lambda := \ker (A - \lambda \, \mathrm{Id})\). By Theorem 53 \(H_\lambda \) is closed and \(\rho \)-invariant. By irreducibility of \(\rho \), the only closed invariant subspaces are \(\bot \) and \(\top \); since \(H_\lambda \) is non-zero, \(H_\lambda = \top \), i.e. \(H_\lambda = H\). Thus \(A v = \lambda v\) for every \(v \in H\), i.e. \(A = \lambda \cdot \mathrm{Id}\).
