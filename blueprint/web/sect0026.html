Let \(\rho _1 : \texttt{UnitaryRep G $V_1$}\), \(\rho _2 : \texttt{UnitaryRep G $V_2$}\) both finite-dimensional and irreducible with \(\rho _1 \not\cong \rho _2\). Then for all \(u_1, w_1 \in V_1\) and \(u_2, w_2 \in V_2\),

\[ \int _G \pi _{u_1, w_1}^{\rho _1}(g)\, \overline{\pi _{u_2, w_2}^{\rho _2}(g)}\, d\mu _G(g) = 0. \]
Proof ▶

Define \(T : V_1 \to V_2\) (note: finite-dimensional) by \(T u := \int _G \pi _{u, w_1}^{\rho _1}(g) \cdot \rho _2(g^{-1}) w_2\, d\mu _G(g)\). Then \(T\) is bounded (finite-dimensional, hence automatic) and an intertwiner \(\rho _1 \to \rho _2\) (verify by changing variables in the integral, using right-invariance). Compactness is automatic in finite dimensions. By Theorem 56, \(T = 0\). Then \(\langle T u_1, u_2\rangle _{V_2} = 0\), which expands to the stated integral being zero.
