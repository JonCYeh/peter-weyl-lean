If \(W' \leq L^2(G)\) is a finite-dim \(\rho ^R\)-invariant subspace and \(u, v \in W'\), then \(\pi _{u, v}^{\rho ^R|_{W'}}\) is a matrix coefficient of \(G\) in the sense of Definition 57, with the rep being \(\rho ^R|_{W'} : \texttt{UnitaryRep G W'}\).

Proof ▶

Tautological from the definitions: a matrix coefficient is a function on \(G\), the rep is the restriction.

Theorem 77 \(\mathcal M\) separates points

Lean declarations

  • UnitaryRep.matrixCoeffSet_separatesPoints

For every pair \(g_1 \neq g_2\) in \(G\) there exists \(f \in \mathcal M\) with \(f(g_1) \neq f(g_2)\).

Proof ▶

Apply Theorem 75 with \(g_0 := g_1 g_2^{-1}\) to get a finite-dim irreducible \(W'\) with \(\rho ^R(g_0)|_{W'} \neq \mathrm{Id}\). Pick \(u \in W'\) with \(\rho ^R(g_0) u \neq u\); pick \(v \in W'\) with \(\langle \rho ^R(g_0) u - u, v\rangle \neq 0\). Then \(\pi _{u, v}^{\rho ^R|_{W'}}(g_0) \neq \pi _{u,v}^{\rho ^R|_{W'}}(1_G)\). Right-translating: define \(u' := \rho ^R(g_2) u\). Then by the matrix-coefficient-translation identity \(\pi _{u', v}^{\rho ^R|_{W'}}(g) = \pi _{u, v}^{\rho ^R|_{W'}}(g g_2)\), giving \(\pi _{u', v}(g_1) \neq \pi _{u', v}(g_2)\). This is an element of \(\mathcal M\) via Theorem 76.
