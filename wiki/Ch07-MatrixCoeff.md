## Definition

For a finite-dim continuous unitary representation $\rho : \texttt{UnitaryRep G V}$ and $u, v \in V$,
$$
\pi_{u,v}^\rho : G \to \mathbb C, \qquad \pi_{u,v}^\rho(g) := \langle \rho(g) u, v\rangle_V.
$$

Continuity (`matrixCoeff_continuous`) and boundedness $|\pi_{u,v}^\rho(g)| \leq \|u\|\|v\|$ (`matrixCoeff_bounded`) are immediate. So every matrix coefficient lives in $C(G, \mathbb C)$ and hence in $L^2(G)$ via $\iota_{C\to L^2}$.

## The algebra structure

The matrix-coefficient set $\mathcal M \subseteq C(G,\mathbb C)$ is closed under sum, scalar multiple, product, complex conjugation, and contains the constant $1$. Concretely:

| Operation on $C(G)$ | Realized as | Lean |
|---|---|---|
| Sum | Direct-sum rep | `UnitaryRep.matrixCoeff_add_eq_directSum` |
| Scalar | Same rep, scaled $u$ | `UnitaryRep.smul_matrixCoeff` |
| Product | Tensor-product rep | `UnitaryRep.matrixCoeff_mul_eq_tensor` |
| Conjugation | Contragredient (dual) rep | `UnitaryRep.matrixCoeff_conj_eq_contragredient` |
| The constant $1$ | Trivial rep | `UnitaryRep.matrixCoeff_one_eq_trivial` |

So
$$
\mathcal A := \mathrm{StarSubalgebra}_{\mathbb C}\, C(G,\mathbb C) \text{ generated by } \mathcal M \;=\; \mathrm{span}_{\mathbb C}\,\mathcal M
$$
(already closed under multiplication and conjugation), and contains the constants. That last sentence is the only thing chapter 9 needs from this chapter — once we know $\mathcal A = \mathrm{span}\,\mathcal M$ is a unital $\ast$-subalgebra of $C(G,\mathbb C)$, we can hit it with Stone–Weierstrass.

## What's blocked

The 5 sorries cluster around two definitions:

1. **Tensor product of unitary reps** (`def:tensor-rep`, `UnitaryRep.tensor`) — Mathlib's `TensorProduct` is over modules; bundling the inner product and the resulting unitary structure is the work. `area:tensor` label.
2. **Contragredient** (`def:contragredient`, `UnitaryRep.contragredient`) — identifying $V^*$ with the conjugate Hilbert space $\overline V$ in a usable way; `matrixCoeff_conj_eq_contragredient` follows once that identification is in place.
