## Statement

$$
\mathcal A := \mathrm{StarSubalgebra}_{\mathbb C}\,C(G,\mathbb C)\text{ generated by matrix coefficients} \;\text{is dense in } C(G,\mathbb C).
$$

So:
$$
\overline{\mathcal A} = C(G, \mathbb C) \quad\text{(uniform closure)}
$$
and as a consequence, matrix coefficients are dense in $L^2(G)$ via $\iota_{C\to L^2}$.

## How

This is the only chapter that's just an invocation. Two ingredients:

1. **$\mathcal A$ separates points of $G$**: for $g_1 \neq g_2$, the separation theorem (Ch. 8) applied to $g_1 g_2^{-1} \neq 1_G$ gives a finite-dim irreducible $\rho$ with $\rho(g_1 g_2^{-1}) \neq \mathrm{Id}$, hence $\rho(g_1) \neq \rho(g_2)$, hence some matrix coefficient takes different values at $g_1$ and $g_2$.
2. **Stone–Weierstrass for complex $C^*$-subalgebras on a compact Hausdorff space.** Mathlib's `StoneWeierstrass.starSubalgebra_topologicalClosure_eq_top_of_separatesPoints` (or its current name) is exactly what's wanted.
